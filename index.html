We meet the first and third Friday of every month, alternating between Manchester Coffee Co. and Awesome Inc.

Why this exists

Claude & Coffee exists to connect people in the Lexington metro area with peer support in learning to use AI.

What happens at a meetup

Our meetups are informal, unconference-style events: attendees show up with topic ideas for discussion, we put them on post-its on a board, and quickly plan the morning.

Great sessions include:

  • Show-and-tell: share how you’re actually using AI.
  • Helpdesk: bring a challenge and ask the group for help.
  • Presentations: discuss challenges or solutions in your industry or occupation.
  • Open discussions: dive in with fellow humans on a hot topic.

We have room for parallel sessions when the room is big enough. Present, ask questions, or just listen.

The group is open to anyone — students and professionals, poets and quants, veterans who’ve lived through an AI winter or novices just starting to experiment with ChatGPT.
